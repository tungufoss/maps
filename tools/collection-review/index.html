Saving, privacy, and backups

Your reactions and notes are saved only in this browser, on this device. Clearing browser data or using another browser can hide or remove them. Export a backup regularly. Your reviews are not sent to the public website or to the creators. The original-gallery pane loads the creator’s website directly; its usual cookies and network requests may apply.

Import merges your backup, keeping the newer version of each review. “Export design brief” turns your positively reviewed collections and chosen features into a Markdown brief you can share in this chat.

Keep using the same file location or local preview address so the browser can find its saved reviews.
